Easytv Play

Privacy policy

Effective 3 November 2026. This is the public policy for the iPhone app.

Easytv Play is a media player made by Soner Altın. The app hosts no content. You provide it by entering your own Xtream server details or an M3U link.

Data the app can store

Email address, only if you sign in with Apple. It identifies the account.

Sync data: favorites, watch history, and the stream source you enter (server address, username, and password). That data is linked to the account so it can sync across your devices.

Where it is stored

Sync data is stored on Supabase, in the EU/Ireland region, in rows only that account can access, and sent over HTTPS. The login session and the stream password are also kept in the iPhone Keychain.

What the app does not do

The app has no ads and does not use data to track you across other companies’ apps and websites. It does not sell personal information or share it for marketing.

Delete the account

Account → Delete Account permanently removes the account and the cloud data stored for it.

Children

Easytv Play is not directed at children.

This website

soneraltin.com uses Google Analytics to count page visits. That measurement stays on the website. It does not receive your playlist, stream password, or watch history from the app.

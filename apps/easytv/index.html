Connect

Xtream Codes or an M3U link

Sign in with Apple, then add the source you are allowed to use. Favorites and history can follow that account across your devices.

The login screen states the limit in plain words: this is a media player, and the subscription has to be one you have the rights to play.

Live TV

Channels, grouped the way the playlist groups them

Search, switch category, and start a channel. The player opens full screen. What you see depends on the stream your provider sends.

Guide

What is on now, and what is next

When the playlist includes a guide, the channel sheet shows what is on now and what comes next. The bell on a later show sets a reminder on the iPhone, five minutes before it starts. The alert names the channel and the programme. Tapping it opens that channel. The reminder stays on the phone. It does not go through a server, and it needs notification permission. A show that is already inside that five-minute window cannot take a reminder.

Movies

A library, not a store

Films from the playlist sit in categories, with a detail page for the title, the runtime, and play. If the source offers a file, it can be downloaded to the iPhone.

Series

Seasons, episodes, and a place to resume

Search the series list, open a season, and continue an episode from the minute you left it.

Downloads

Keep a title on the phone

Downloads shows what is saved and how far the file has come in. Playback of a saved title does not need the stream to be open.

Player only

Easytv Play does not sell a lineup

Your list. Nothing added behind it.

The app does not include channels, films, or series. It plays what an Xtream server or an M3U playlist already gives you. Picture, guide data, and catch-up depend on that source.

Languages

Easytv Play follows the language of the iPhone. The interface is in Turkish, English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, and Simplified Chinese. Another iPhone language falls back to Turkish, which is the language the app was written in.

There are no ads and no cross-app tracking. An optional one-time tip in the app supports development. It unlocks no extra player.

Lock Screen and Dynamic Island

While a channel, film, or episode is playing, the iPhone keeps it on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. The card shows the title and the artwork. Pause, jump back or forward 15 seconds, and AirPlay sit on the Lock Screen. The Island shows the artwork and that audio is playing. Leave the app and the controls stay there.

IPTV player questions

Does Easytv Play include channels?

No. You bring a playlist you are allowed to use. The app does not sell, host, or add a channel lineup of its own.

Xtream or M3U?

Both. Xtream Codes uses a server address, username, and password. M3U uses a playlist link. The library screens are the same either way.

Are there ads?

No ads and no tracking. A one-time tip is optional and does not unlock a separate version of the player.

Can I control playback from the Lock Screen?

Yes. While something is playing, the Lock Screen shows the title, artwork, pause, 15-second skip, and AirPlay. The Dynamic Island shows the artwork and that audio is playing. These stay up when you leave the app.

Can Easytv Play remind me when a live show starts?

Yes. On the guide, the bell next to an upcoming programme schedules a notification five minutes before it begins. The alert shows the channel and the title. Tap it and that channel opens. The reminder is stored on the iPhone. iOS must allow notifications, and the show has to be more than five minutes away.

Which languages does Easytv Play use?

Turkish, English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Dutch, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, and Simplified Chinese. The iPhone’s language picks the one you see. Anything else uses Turkish.

Can I delete the account and the saved playlist?

Yes. Account → Delete Account removes the cloud user and the synced favorites, history, and stream source. The privacy policy has the detail.

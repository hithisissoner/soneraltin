YOUR PLAYLIST

An ad-free player for your own M3U or Xtream Codes playlist — live TV, movies and series in one place.

Easytv Play does not provide channels, subscriptions or content. Add only a source you are allowed to use.

Favorites, downloads, EPG, resume, subtitles, Face ID lock, and Sign in with Apple sync.

  • Download movies and series to your iPhone or iPad
  • See the program guide (EPG) on live TV
  • Choose subtitles

Questions

No ads. No third-party tracking.

Baseline - Sleep Debt

Privacy policy

Effective 19 November 2026. This is the public policy for the iPhone app. The same text is available in the app under Settings → Privacy Policy.

Baseline is a wellness app made by Soner Altın. It is not a medical device and it does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition.

Health data on this iPhone

On this iPhone, with your permission, Baseline reads heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, and date of birth from Apple Health. Those readings are used to show recovery and sleep on this phone. Baseline does not write health data back to Apple Health. Health readings are not sold, and they are not sent to an advertising network.

Apple Intelligence summaries run on your device. They use the numbers already on screen. Those summaries are not stored on our server.

You can turn off Health access in the Settings app, under Privacy & Security → Health.

Account

Sign in with Apple creates an account at our database host, Supabase. That account can store your email if Apple provides one, your sleep schedule, and the mood check-ins you save. A mood check-in can include the mood, optional tags, an optional note, and a copy of that day’s sleep efficiency, resting heart rate, and heart rate variability.

Sign Out stays on this phone and leaves the account in place. Delete Account removes the Supabase user. The sleep schedule and mood log stored for that user are removed with it, and this phone signs out. Apple Health access stays on this iPhone until you turn it off in the Health app.

Purchases

Premium is an optional one-time Apple purchase that supports development. It unlocks no extra features. The free app and Premium show the same recovery tools. Purchases are handled by Apple. Baseline does not store payment details.

What we do not do

Baseline does not sell personal information. It does not use health data, account data, or mood notes for advertising or tracking across other companies’ apps and websites.

Contact

Questions about this policy or a request to delete an account can be sent to hello@soneraltin.com. You can also delete the account inside the app, in Settings.
